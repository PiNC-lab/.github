# PiNC Lab

## For People & Planet

**Evidence and innovation for nature-based solutions that work in Africa.**

The **People in Nature & Climate (PiNC) Lab** is a research group based at the **African Climate & Development Initiative (ACDI) at the University of Cape Town (UCT)**.

We explore how to work with nature to promote human and ecosystem health, while supporting climate mitigation and adaptation actions that reduce climate risk in Africa.

### Our Mission

Researchers at the PiNC Lab collaborate with partners to generate evidence, ideas and guidance that enables nature-based actions for local and planetary health.

Our work supports solutions that are effective and equitable across scales, with a particular focus on sub-Saharan Africa.

### Our Approach

Nature-based solutions (NbS) are actions that leverage the power of nature and natural processes to address diverse societal and environmental issues.

These actions enable us to protect, restore and manage ecosystems, and to address challenges of food and water security, climate change, land degradation and biodiversity loss.

Our work aims to develop nature-based solutions that are financially viable, inclusive of communities and local knowledge, and effective in safeguarding livelihoods and sustainable futures.
